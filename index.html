VibruSensor · Evidence map

Connecting the puzzle

VibruSensor is a synthesis: event-camera papers establish full-field vibration measurement; machinery literature tells us which signatures matter; localisation work defines the diagnostic target; and control literature shows why the measured mechanical response can become a supervisory feedback signal.

01 · Full-field sensing

Dorn et al. — frequency + mode shapes

Demonstrated event-camera full-field vibration measurement and operational modal analysis.

We borrow: one event sensor can recover a spatial vibration field rather than only a point signal.

02 · Quantitative motion

Kato & Miyamoto — subpixel displacement

Combined event tracking and low-rank completion to reconstruct quantitative subpixel vibration and mode-shape change.

We borrow: the route from event activity to a quantitative displacement field.
Kato & Miyamoto 2026 ↗
03 · Machinery frequency maps

Aitsam et al. — per-pixel frequency mapping

Applied event-based frequency mapping to machinery and used spatial frequency changes to flag abnormal operation.

We borrow: the practical machinery-facing frequency-map baseline.
Aitsam et al. 2025 ↗
04 · Motion / phase

Niwa + Zhou — richer vibration state

Recover amplitude, frequency, phase, subtle motion fields, spectra, and vibration modes from event-based measurements.

We borrow: the methods needed to move beyond a frequency-only map.
Niwa et al. 2026 ↗Zhou et al. 2025 ↗
05 · Diagnostic evidence

Machinery literature — frequency alone is not enough

Direction, location, relative phase, and response to operating condition distinguish faults that share spectral lines.

We borrow: the variables that turn a spatial vibration map into diagnostic evidence.
Mishani et al. 2015 ↗Mogal & Lalwani 2015 ↗
06 · Localisation target

Zhao et al. — qualitative frame-camera localisation

A 1000-fps frame camera with motion magnification showed which bearing seat moved and in which direction for seeded faults.

We borrow: the machine-level target — then make localisation event-based and quantitatively scored.
Zhao et al. 2023 ↗
07 · Controller changes vibration

Szabat + Kai — control changes physical motion

Controller feedback can excite or damp torsional vibration and can change which physical component carries motion.

We borrow: the motivation for using full-field vibration as supervisory feedback.
Szabat et al. 2007 ↗Kai et al. 2018 ↗
08 · Feasible test bed

Hafez & Dhaouadi — AUS flexible two-mass drive

The identified flexible drive has a torsional mode in the tens of hertz, overlapping published event-camera vibration measurements.

We borrow: a realistic experimental bridge from vibrometry into vibration-aware control.
Hafez & Dhaouadi 2023 ↗

Already demonstrated separately

  • Event-camera frequency and mode-shape recovery
  • Subpixel displacement in controlled setups
  • Per-pixel event frequency maps on machinery
  • Qualitative localisation with frame cameras
  • Controller-dependent vibration on compliant systems

What VibruSensor connects

  • One event camera viewing a real machine as a spatial field
  • Frequency + relative phase + motion / amplitude
  • Seeded faults with position withheld from the algorithm
  • Quantitative localisation against synchronized references
  • Extension from diagnosis into supervisory vibration-aware control
